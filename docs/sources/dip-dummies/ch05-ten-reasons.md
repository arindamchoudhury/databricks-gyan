## The ten reasons

1. **Eliminates data & AI silos** — one place unifying DW, data lake, BI, OLTP, ML, and GenAI stacks.
2. **Enhances data & AI security** — robust security to protect sensitive data and meet compliance.
3. **Accelerates application & agent development** — rapidly build/deploy/govern AI apps and composable agents on your own data.
4. **Democratizes data & AI** — business users/analysts/execs query and visualize via NL chatbots + self-service dashboards, without sacrificing governance.
5. **Finds more intelligent insights** — platform learns your data, metadata, usage, and feedback → agents that understand company-specific definitions and rules.
6. **Accelerates tasks via automation & AI assistance** — automates pipelines and infra management, reducing manual effort and error.
7. **Better collaboration** — easy sharing of insights, code, results across teams.
8. **Massive, cost-effective scale** — separates storage and compute; structured/semi/unstructured all in one platform.
9. **Single, trusted source of truth** — centralizes business semantics, metrics, lineage, metadata so everyone works from governed data.
10. **Improves ROI** — consolidating management + analytics cuts cost and shifts from reactive reporting to proactive AI-driven decisions.
